Where does the Web come from?

The Social Web

Web 1.0 is often described as the "read only" web. It was characterized by static web pages with very limited user interaction. Most websites served as online brochures or repositories of inforfmation, where users could view content but could not easily contribute or modify it.

This early stage of the Web was dominated by institutional and corporate websites that provided information but offered little opportunity for collaboration or participation.
